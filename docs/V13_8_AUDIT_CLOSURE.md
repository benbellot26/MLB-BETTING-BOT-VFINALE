# V13.8 — 52-point audit closure

V13.8 closes the engineering obligations from the two consolidated audits without weakening any native-live or point-in-time evidence gate.

## Closure semantics

A point has two independent states:

- **Engineering closed**: the provider, feature, model, validator, monitor, or fail-closed gate exists in code and is covered by tests.
- **Evidence closed**: the required authenticated point-in-time sample size and out-of-sample performance condition have actually been satisfied.

A research implementation is never promoted merely because reconstructed historical data is abundant. `HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTED_FREE` remains research-only and cannot satisfy native-live promotion floors.

## New V13.8 layers

- advanced offense/starter/lineup/pitch-mix/bullpen/opener/context feature functions;
- GLM, GAM, portable boosted-stump, empirical-Bayes hierarchy, learned ensemble and meta-model research candidates;
- strict multi-season walk-forward, naive baselines, ablations, paired bootstrap confidence intervals, calibration and subgroup diagnostics;
- learned bookmaker-weight and extra-innings candidates with explicit minimum-N gates;
- dynamic calibration, inning-level profile, contextual score dispersion and season regimes as research-only layers;
- critical starter/lineup change signatures permitting corrected Discord republication without duplicate spam;
- DuckDB + Parquet analytical research copies, SHA-256 checksums, dataset/version/feature-contract fingerprints and reproducibility metadata;
- provider-health history, feature-drift alerts and a static HTML health dashboard;
- a machine-readable 52-point closure registry at `data/v138_audit_closure.json`.

## Evidence gates intentionally retained

Rich native promotion, calibration activation, posterior Sharp influence, empirical interval coverage, learned bookmaker weights, extra-innings prior, CLV/model-market proof and inning-level learning remain inactive until their real evidence thresholds are met. Their architecture is closed; their statistical proof is not fabricated.
